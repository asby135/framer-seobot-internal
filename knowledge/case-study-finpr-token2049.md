The Strategy: Pre-Conference Outreach

Targeting Web3 Decision-Makers

FINPR leveraged a ready-made Web3 decision-makers database to identify prospects attending Token 2049:

Blockchain project founders needing marketing support

C-level executives at crypto startups

Business development leaders at established Web3 companies

Two-Message Sequence

FINPR created a strategic sequence combining business development with immediate value:

Message 1 - Value Introduction: "Hi {name}, will you be attending TOKEN2049 Singapore? I'm with FINPR, a Web3 marketing agency helping projects boost visibility through PR, KOL campaigns, and community growth. Here's our pitch deck if you'd like to take a look: https://finpr.me/pd. Would be glad to explore potential collaboration."

Message 2 - Follow-up (3 days later): "Hey {name}, just following up! If you're planning to attend TOKEN2049, we have tickets available at a discounted price of $499 (the current official price is $999 now). Would that be interesting?"

Implementation: Multi-Account Distribution

FINPR utilized four different Telegram accounts to scale their outreach, enabling them to increase message volume beyond single-account limits while maintaining deliverability and a personal touch.

Deals were automatically created in the CRM when prospects replied to their outreach messages, and an 'unread' mark appeared in the deal card, enabling the team to continue the conversation manually.
