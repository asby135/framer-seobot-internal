The Challenge: Fragmented Tools and Unreliable Systems

We spoke with Yakov Kovalev , one of Lead Sniper's co-founders and a former Tech manager at Yandex, about their transformation.

"We had our own solution that wasn't quite satisfying us in terms of reliability and features," Yakov explains. "There was a lot of context switching - it wasn't all executed in one window. It was inefficient."

Before CRMChat, Lead Sniper's workflow involved:

Google Sheets for prospect data management

Desktop Telegram client for manual messaging

Custom scripts for automation attempts

AmoCRM for deal tracking and collaboration

"Everything was scattered across different tools," Yakov continues. "We could have continued developing our own solution further, but it would have been quite complex. When we found CRMChat, it solved exactly what we needed."

The Search: Evaluating Multiple Solutions

Lead Sniper's search for a better solution was thorough and methodical.

"I Googled, asked acquaintances, and compared several solutions," Yakov recalls. "There was CRMChat and some other big professional tool. We also examined open-source solutions, but they required significant customization. Then there were paid solutions - I think one was called Mango or something similar."

The breakthrough came through their network: "Everything was a bit off - not quite right for our specific Telegram goals. Then an acquaintance wrote that CRMChat is a good solution. That was the deciding factor. We went to try it."
