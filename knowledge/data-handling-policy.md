# CRMChat Data Handling Policy

> 📖 Source: https://crmchat.ai/data-handling
> Version 1.0 | Effective date: Oct 10, 2024

## Overview

CRMchat.ai delivers CRM and Outreach functionality for Telegram. We've built our system with maximum security and minimal exposure of our users' data in mind.

## Data Handling Standards

Crmchat.ai — CRM and Outreach functionality for Telegram, owned by Hintsflow Inc. — adheres to the following strict data protection standards, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all customer data processed through its platform:

- **No permanent message storage.** We do not permanently store any Telegram messages or customer communications.
- **End-to-end encryption.** All data passing through our infrastructure is fully encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest using strong, industry-grade encryption (AES-256).
- **No raw data in analytics or logs.** No raw customer data is used for analytics or logging.
- **Customer-controlled deletion.** Clients retain full ownership and control of their data. A client can disconnect their workspace or delete their instance at any time, and all associated data is deleted instantly and irreversibly.
- **EU hosting available on request.** At customers' request, workspaces can be hosted on EU-based server infrastructure.
