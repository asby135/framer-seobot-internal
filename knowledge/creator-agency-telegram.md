# Telegram CRM for Content Creator Agencies (OnlyFans / Model Management)

> Source: https://crmchat.fan/

## Overview

CRMChat is a Telegram-native CRM for content creator agencies — teams that manage multiple creators/models, coordinate chatters, and sell PPV (pay-per-view) content directly in Telegram DMs. Manage your entire creator portfolio from a single Telegram dashboard.

Headline metrics: 300+ creator accounts connected, 100K+ CRM leads created, "10x increase in response time" for agencies that move fan conversations into a coordinated Telegram inbox.

## Multi-Account Telegram Chat Management

Connect all model accounts to CRMChat, invite chatters to the workspace, and manage every client conversation on Telegram from a single dashboard.

**Smart account switching.** For each client, CRMChat automatically selects the account that was used with them earlier — so a chatter never messages a customer from the wrong model account. Smart account switching prevents mix-ups across a large portfolio.

**Scale chatters.** Add an unlimited number of chatters to the workspace.

## Separate Chatter Role (Security-First)

Chatters get a limited-permission role: restricted account actions to keep model accounts safe and prevent client data from being compromised. Admins keep full control; chatters get only what they need to do the conversations.

## Direct Notifications

The responsible chatter receives a notification directly in their own Telegram the moment a customer messages — speed matters, and faster replies mean more sales.

## Telegram PPV Sales Bot

Sell PPV (pay-per-view) content directly on Telegram with a seamless payment experience and zero chargebacks. Creators and agencies can withdraw earnings to a crypto wallet right inside Telegram.

## AI-Powered Response Automation

Connect AI agents to creators' Telegram accounts. The AI responds to client questions using the knowledge base you provide, keeping conversations active 24/7 even when the team is offline.

**How it works:** AI is connected to the models' Telegram accounts. When customers ask questions, the AI answers from the knowledge base you supply. It only answers when confident — otherwise it waits for human (chatter) intervention. This keeps conversations active around the clock without risking a wrong or off-brand reply. The AI's tone and personality are customizable per model.
