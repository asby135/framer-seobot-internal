Hintsflow Inc., d/b/a as CRMchat ("CRMchat", "we", "us", "our") provides the development of a Customer Relationship Management application allowing our users to efficiently capture, organize and search contact information, personal notes and tasks.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us via email at support@hints.so.

## 11. Chrome Extension — Telegram Group Parser

### Data Collection and Storage

When you use our Telegram Group Parser Chrome extension, we collect and store the following information.

**CSV Export Files.** We store copies of the CSV files you generate when parsing Telegram groups. These files may contain:

- Telegram usernames
- Full names of group members
- User IDs
- Profile status information (photo status, stories status, online status)
- Gender information (if provided by users)

**Purpose of Storage.** We store these CSV files to:

- Provide you with access to your parsing history
- Enable seamless integration with CRMChat's outreach features
- Improve our service and understand usage patterns
- Troubleshoot technical issues when requested

**Data Retention.** CSV parsing data is retained indefinitely unless you request deletion.

**Your Control.** You can request deletion of your stored CSV data at any time by contacting ask@hints.so or support@hints.so.

**Security.** All stored CSV files are encrypted and stored securely on our servers. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

**Third-Party Sharing.** We do not sell, rent, or share the contents of your CSV files with third parties, except:

- When required by law
- With your explicit consent
- When you choose to import the data into CRMChat for outreach campaigns

**Data Source.** The extension only collects publicly visible information from Telegram groups where you are already a member. No private messages or unauthorized data are accessed.
