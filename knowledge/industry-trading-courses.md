## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I sell a trading course on Telegram?

Most trading educators drive traffic to a free Telegram channel or group, build trust with market updates and analysis, then reach out to subscribers in DM. CRMChat automates that last step: it syncs every new subscriber to a CRM and sends them a personalized DM, so you can sell your course, mentorship, or VIP access at scale.

### Can I DM my Telegram channel subscribers?

Telegram channels are broadcast-only, so you can't message subscribers from the channel itself. With Telegram Channel Sync, subscribers become CRM contacts that you can enroll in automated DM campaigns sent from your connected Telegram accounts.

### How does the automated DM to new subscribers work?

Create a dynamic outreach campaign with "Leads from CRM" as the audience. Every time Channel Sync adds a new member, they're automatically enrolled and receive your message from one of your connected accounts — no manual exports or uploads.

### How will my sales team know when a lead replies?

As soon as a subscriber responds, your sales manager gets a notification in the CRMChat Telegram bot. They can reply right away, move the deal through your pipeline, and close the sale.

### Does it work with private channels and group chats?

Yes. Add the CRMChat bot as an admin to your private or public channel or group, and it will sync existing members and track every new join and leave in real time.

### Can I send DMs from multiple Telegram accounts?

Yes. Connect several Telegram accounts to your workspace and CRMChat distributes new leads between them, helping you reach more subscribers while keeping each account's daily volume safe.

### Will subscribers get duplicate messages?

No. Contacts are matched by Telegram ID and username, so each subscriber is added to the CRM once and enrolled in the campaign once. Bots and deleted accounts are filtered out automatically.

### Can I tag leads by channel or funnel?

Yes. Map CRM custom properties to join and leave events — for example, set "Source = Free Signals Channel" when someone joins and "Status = Churned" when they leave. That makes it easy to run different DM scripts for different channels.

### How many subscribers can I sync?

There's no hard limit. Large channels are synced in batches with built-in rate limiting, so you can import thousands of existing subscribers and keep capturing new ones in real time.

### How much does it cost?

The Channel Sync bot is free to start — connect your channel and sync subscribers at no cost. You'll need a paid plan once you go beyond 50 CRM contacts or want to connect additional Telegram accounts for outreach.
