What is Telegram account warmup?

Account warmup is the process of gradually building activity history on a new Telegram account before starting outreach. It includes sending messages, joining channels, reacting to posts, and other normal user behaviors. This helps Telegram recognize your account as legitimate, reducing the risk of bans when you scale messaging.
