# crmchat Pricing Plans

crmchat offers four plans: Starter (free), Personal, Team, and Agency. Billing is monthly, quarterly or yearly: quarterly saves 15% and yearly saves 30%, on both seats and Telegram account connections. Personal and Team come with a 1-week trial.

Source: https://crmchat.ai/pricing (checked 22 October 2026).

### How Team pricing works

Team pricing has two parts: a per-seat fee for each team member, plus a flat fee per Telegram account connection. The first connection is included free. A connection can be a Telegram account crmchat provides or your own.

| Item | Monthly | Quarterly | Yearly |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Team member (seat) | $49/month | $41/month | $34/month |
| Telegram account connection | 1st free, then $10/month | 1st free, then $8/month | 1st free, then $7/month |

Example: 2 seats and 3 Telegram accounts on monthly billing cost 2 × $49 + 2 × $10 = $118 per month.

Estimated outreach capacity uses account safety limits of 10 cold and 50 warm leads per account per day.
