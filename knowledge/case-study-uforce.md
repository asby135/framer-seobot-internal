The Challenge: Manual Outreach at Scale

We spoke with Olga, uForce's lead project manager, about their transformation.

"We are an agency that handles Telegram outreach projects for our clients, and we were doing everything manually," Olga explains. "Each contact had to be messaged individually by hand. We had a task to automate our messaging processes because it was becoming impossible to scale."

uForce specializes in B2B lead generation for diverse clients - from SaaS platforms to payment processors targeting large retail chains.

"We were exploring other no-code solutions when Roman told us about CRMChat at exactly the right moment," Olga recalls. "CRMChat literally came at the perfect time - we evaluated it and decided to switch immediately."

Implementation: Project-by-Project Migration

Starting Gradually

"We began using CRMChat in early June, implementing it project by project," Olga says. "We started by adding projects one by one at first. Now we have 10 projects set up, and practically all our projects that can be run through CRMChat are managed through the platform."

This phased approach allowed uForce to test capabilities while minimizing risk to existing client relationships.

Statistics of the Telegram cold outreach campaign targeting Tech Founders for one of uForce’s customers (Global Payroll Platform). The sequence consisted of just two messages: a Company Intro and a nudge two days later.

Campaign Structure

For each client project, uForce uses 2-3 dedicated Telegram accounts. "We don't use client accounts because we use dedicated warmed-up accounts for better delivery rates," Olga explains.

Their account preparation involves a week-long manual warming process, followed by conservative sending limits of 25 messages per account per day to maintain long-term deliverability.

The Results: Complete Operational Transformation

Eliminated Manual Work

"CRMChat has eliminated the manual work of sending first and second messages - that's now automated," Olga shares. "Our team can focus on strategy, campaign optimization, and client management instead of repetitive messaging tasks."

Mobile Accessibility

"One thing our team really noticed is the convenience of having all projects at hand on your phone," Olga explains. "Even if you step away somewhere, you can check through your phone and see if someone responded. You're not so tied to your computer anymore."

When requested, Olga was able to easily add a customer’s Telegram account to their CRMChat workspace, allowing the client to track campaign performance.
