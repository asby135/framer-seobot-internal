The Strategy: Transparency as Growth

Instead of hiding our methods, I made our entire workflow transparent. The sequence walked prospects through exactly how we found them and how they could replicate the process for their own businesses.

The Clay + CRMChat Workflow

Step 1: Prospect Research in Clay

I used Clay's filtering system to find Web3 companies:

Company size : 11-50 employees

Industry keywords : Web3, Crypto, DeFi

Contact enrichment : Mobile phones for Telegram conversion

Step 2: Decision-Maker Identification

Clay's filters helped identify the right contacts:

Job titles : Owner, Partner, C-suite, VP, Director

Contact method : using Clay's enrichment, I enriched my prospect list with Mobile Phones, which I then converted to Telegram usernames using CRMChat tools.

This process yielded 175 qualified Telegram prospects in 30 minutes.

Why This Approach Worked

Transparency Built Trust : Sharing our exact workflow demonstrated confidence and gave prospects immediate value.

Educational Content as Sales : The sequence doubled as a tutorial, teaching prospects while showcasing our platform.

Meta Marketing Effect : Using CRMChat to promote CRMChat created natural conversation starters and proved our effectiveness.

Industry-Specific Targeting : Web3 companies already understand Telegram's value for business communication.

Use Your Own Product : The most authentic way to demonstrate value is using your tool for your own growth.

Share Your Methods : In a crowded market, transparency can be more powerful than generic sales messages.

Teach While You Sell : Educational outreach builds credibility and may lead to future conversions even if prospects don't buy immediately.

Meta Campaigns Work : Self-referential marketing creates authenticity that resonates with prospects.
