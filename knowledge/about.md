2019: GetIntent

Based in Brooklyn, New York, we previously built the AI ad platform Getintent, scaled it to $16M ARR, and sold it in 2019.

2021: Hints

Our new venture began in 2021 as Hints, with a $2.5M investment from Altair, Flashpoint, and angel investors. Initially, Hints was an AI Assistant for CRMs, working with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive clients.

2024:CRMChat

In 2024, we launched the CRM on Telegram, focusing on Web3 companies. Our first client was Solana Superteam Germany. They helped us tailor the product to the needs of the Web3 community, and we’re deeply grateful for their support.

Why Telegram?
